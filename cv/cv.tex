% ================= RESEARCH INTERESTS =================
\cvsection{Research Interests}
How people learn about the world and use what they have learned to guide value-based decisions,
studied through the interaction of memory and decision-making and formalized with computational
models. Related interests include event memory, reinforcement learning, naturalistic stimuli, and
functional neuroimaging.
